# Misconduct / Governance Unified Research Lifecycle

## Canonical Edge

`misconduct-overreaction-recovery` is the active Research OS Edge for company-internal misconduct and governance shock research.

`known-bad-event-repricing` is retained only as deprecated provenance. Its useful hypothesis is not discarded: formal-event repricing is measured inside the canonical misconduct case as a secondary diagnostic route.

## One incident, one case timeline

1. Initial public misconduct/governance shock.
2. PIT classification of economic damage, accounting impact, actor separability, management dependence, regulatory/litigation risk, brand damage and recurrence risk.
3. Initial executable-price reaction and benchmark-adjusted drawdown.
4. Formal events such as press conferences, shareholder meetings, committee reports, sanctions, judgments, corrections or resignations.
5. At each formal event, separate already-known facts from genuinely new adverse information.
6. Measure D+1/D+3/D+5 secondary repricing after the formal event as a diagnostic, not as a second incident sample.
7. Follow remediation evidence and business KPIs.
8. Measure D+5/D+20/D+60/D+120 recovery, pre-event reclaim, overshoot, MAE/MFE and permanent impairment.

## Why the former Known-Bad Edge was absorbed

The two active designs shared the same issuer/event universe, information-state timeline, price and benchmark requirements, and most confounders. Separate active Edges would encourage duplicate samples and fragmented conclusions.

No formal `known-bad-event-repricing` samples existed at consolidation time, so no sample or outcome migration is required.

## How secondary repricing is used

Until independently validated with execution and borrow costs, formal-event secondary repricing is a diagnostic input for:

- avoiding premature long entries;
- `WAIT` / `WATCH` reasoning;
- identifying false bargains;
- comparing initial-shock entry versus post-formal-event entry timing;
- explaining why an otherwise recoverable company may experience another short drawdown before mean reversion.

It is not an automatic short or SELL signal.

## Research integrity

The old Edge ID, immutable hypothesis and creation date remain in the registry as deprecated provenance. A single real-world incident must not be counted once under the initial shock route and again as a separate Known-Bad sample.

Sanrio may be used as a calibration seed only after primary evidence, PIT prices, benchmark and confounders are verified. It must not be pre-labelled as a successful recovery case.
